MY STORY

Curiosity became
a direction.

I’m Adeniji Clement Bamikole. My cybersecurity journey began with an interest in how computer systems work and how they can be secured. I wanted to understand what happens behind the screens people use every day, where risks can appear, and how thoughtful security can protect systems and the people who rely on them.

That curiosity led me to study ethical hacking. I’m currently an Ethical Hacking student in TechCrush Cohort 9, building my foundation through training and practical learning. I’m developing Linux basics, web development, and cybersecurity skills step by step, with an emphasis on responsible practice and clear permission.

I’m motivated by the idea that security knowledge can be used to help others. As I learn, I want to document what I try, understand the risks involved, and focus on ways to make systems safer.
